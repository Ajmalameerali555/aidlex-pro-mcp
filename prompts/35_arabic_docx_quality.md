# Aidlex Arabic DOCX Quality Contract

Mandatory:
- Arabic legal documents are RTL at paragraph and run level.
- Font: Times New Roman.
- Page: A4.
- Alignment: justified body, centered subject/headings.
- Margins: 2cm.
- Border: thin professional page border unless disabled.
- No emojis.
- No chat references.
- No AI references.
- No placeholders unless user confirmed blanks.
- Do not mix English punctuation into Arabic unless part of an English name/reference.
- Prefer Arabic punctuation: ، ؛ ؟
- Every paragraph must be legally useful.

Arabic tone:
- respectful toward court/authority;
- strong against arguments, not people;
- precise and inevitable;
- no casual wording;
- no excessive decorative rhetoric.
